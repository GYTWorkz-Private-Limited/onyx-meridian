# PHASE 2 ENTERPRISE CAPABILITIES

Do not redesign existing screens.

Do not change layouts.

Maintain Onyx visual language.

Extend existing functionality with the following enterprise capabilities.

---

## SELF-EVOLVING AI WORKFORCE

Extend Workforce Registry and AgentOps.

Every AI Employee should have:

* Accuracy Score
* Reliability Score
* Eval Pass Rate
* Drift Score
* Cost Efficiency
* Business Impact Score

Agent Lifecycle:

Draft
→ Deploy
→ Observe
→ Evaluate
→ Improve
→ Redeploy

If evaluation scores decline:

* Flag agent
* Trigger retraining recommendation
* Suggest replacement agent
* Escalate to governance if required

Add:

"Self-Evolution Status"

Values:

* Learning
* Stable
* Improving
* Degrading
* Replacement Recommended

## HUMAN APPROVAL FRAMEWORK

Add approval requirements throughout the platform.

Define approval policies:

Low Risk
→ Auto Approve

Medium Risk
→ Manager Approval

High Risk
→ Director Approval

Critical Risk
→ Executive Approval

Mandatory Rule:

Any workflow involving money, spending, contracts, procurement, payments, discounts, or budget allocation must require human approval.

Display:

Approval Required
Approver
Approval Status
Approval History

* Which agents are underperforming?
* Which agents are improving?
* Which agents require replacement?
* Which workflows require approval?
* Why was a decision made?
* What business impact was created?
* What risks were prevented?

The platform should clearly demonstrate governed autonomy, human oversight, self-improving agents, policy enforcement, observability, and enterprise trust.
