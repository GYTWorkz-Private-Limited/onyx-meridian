## 2. The problem

**Section title:** AI exists across the enterprise. But does it truly operate within it?

Most organizations are adopting AI one assistant at a time. Copilots stay trapped inside individual applications. Automations run without business context. Agents are spun up faster than anyone can govern them.

The result isn't intelligence — it's fragmentation. More agents, less control. No one can answer the questions that actually matter:

- Who does this agent report to?
- What systems is it allowed to touch — and under whose authority?
- Who approved that action?
- What did it cost, and was it worth it?

**Pull quote:** The winners in enterprise AI won't be the ones with the most agents. They'll be the ones who can operate and govern them like a real workforce.

---

## 3. The solution

**Section title:** One control plane for your entire AI workforce.

Meridian treats AI agents the way you treat people in an org. You hire them into departments. You give them a role, a scope, and the exact tools they need. You watch what they do, approve what matters, and control what they cost.

Intelligence built into the workflow — not bolted on top.

**Three-step model:**

1. **Hire** — Onboard AI employees into departments with an archetype, a model, and a reasoning level tuned to the work.
2. **Scope** — Grant precise, governed capabilities (Units of Work) instead of unbounded "do-anything" access.
3. **Govern** — Wire in approvals, role-based access, and cost controls, then watch every action stay traceable and auditable.

## 4. Capabilities (feature grid)

### 🗺️ AI Org Chart
A live canvas of your entire AI workforce — every agent, the department it reports to, and what it's working on right now. Structure you can see, not a spreadsheet you have to maintain.

### 🤖 AI Employees
Hire agents with a defined archetype (Analyst, Coordinator, Executor, Advisor, Auditor), a model, and a reasoning level. Each carries its own memory, activity timeline, and chat — so it learns how your business actually operates.

### 🧩 Units of Work
Atomic, governed capabilities an agent can invoke against real systems — an ERP action, a document signature, a ledger update. Each has clear ownership (RACI), vault-secured credentials, and a measurable ROI. Capability without the blast radius.

### 🔗 Connectors & Adapters
Plug into the systems you already run — SAP, Yardi, AppFolio, DocuSign and more — and bring your own agent runtime (Claude, Codex, Cursor, or any HTTP endpoint). LLM-agnostic. Deploy-anywhere.

### 🛡️ Governance & Approvals
Secrets live in a vault. Access is role-based (Member / Dept Head / Admin). Human approvals are wired into the flow where they matter. Every action is controlled, compliant, and auditable.

### 💸 Cost & Effectiveness Control
Model choice and reasoning effort are the real cost levers — Meridian makes both visible and steerable per department, so leaders can align spend to outcomes and measure effectiveness, not just activity.

### 🏢 Multi-Company & Multi-Department
Run isolated, governed workspaces for every business unit or client. Data stays separated; standards stay shared.

### 🚀 Department Onboarding
A guided flow to stand up a new department's AI workforce: select the department, connect its systems, let Meridian discover the Units of Work, map effectiveness and ROI, and publish.

## 5. How it works — two layers, one system

**Section title:** Where work meets enterprise intelligence.

**Employee Intelligence Layer — Co-Work**
AI coworkers embedded directly in the flow of work. They understand workflows and system state, assist users with contextual guidance, and execute strictly within defined boundaries.

**Enterprise Intelligence Layer — Digital Twin**
A live, operational model of your enterprise. Not a dashboard — a model of the business itself, capturing what's happening now and what's likely to happen next.

Together they form a continuous layer of intelligence connecting **knowledge → insight → action**, with every action governed and traced.

## 6. Built on enterprise context

**Section title:** 7 layers of enterprise context.

Meridian builds a continuous, multi-layered understanding of the enterprise — knowing what's happening, why it matters, and what actions are possible.

1. **Domain** — industry rules, standards, constraints
2. **Business** — goals, KPIs, priorities, outcomes
3. **Application** — systems, APIs, workflows
4. **Data** — real-time signals, metrics, events
5. **Organization** — roles, hierarchy, ownership
6. **User** — behavior, intent, preferences
7. **Policy** — governance, compliance, control

## 8. Trust & governance

- **Governed by default** — permissions, approvals, and audit trails across the entire stack.
- **Secrets stay secret** — credentials held in a vault and referenced by capability, never exposed to agents.
- **Traceable & auditable** — a complete activity record of who did what, when, and under whose authority.
- **LLM-agnostic & deploy-anywhere** — modular APIs and SDKs; bring your own models and runtimes.
